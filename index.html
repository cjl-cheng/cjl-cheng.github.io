About

Understanding AI behavior and supporting trustworthiness.

I am an M.Eng. student in Safety Science and Engineering at Beihang University's School of Reliability and Systems Engineering. I hold a B.Eng. in Safety Engineering and a B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics (minor degree), both from Beihang University.

My current focus is on language models. I explore questions about their behavior through model-component analysis, controlled interventions, interpretable failure detection, and formal methods. I am interested in explanations that are faithful to model behavior and useful for assessing it.

Research interests

Questions and approaches

01

How models use information

Studying how model components and computations shape the way language models use information.

02

Why models make mistakes

Investigating model errors through controlled experiments and signals that help assess behavior.

03

Explanations people can assess

Asking when an explanation is supported by model behavior and how it can help people inspect failures.

04

Formal methods for AI

Exploring how precise specifications and verification ideas can support clear, checkable claims about AI behavior.

Research themes

Questions guiding my work

Understanding model behavior

How does model structure affect behavior?

I study how language models use information and how their internal computations relate to success and failure.

I use controlled experiments and interventions to test explanations, while separating observed behavior from claims about underlying mechanisms.

Interpretable failure detection

How can failure detectors support inspection?

I explore ways to detect model errors with evidence that can be inspected and checked.

I assess predictive usefulness alongside the clarity of detector decisions, with attention to where each approach works and where it fails.
