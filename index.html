AI interpretability
and trustworthy AI.

I want to understand how language models use information, why they make mistakes, and how explanations can help people assess and improve their behavior.

I am an M.Eng. student in Safety Science and Engineering at Beihang University’s School of Reliability and Systems Engineering. My current focus is on language-model behavior and errors.

Research interests

How do models use information?

I study how model components and internal computations relate to behavior, using controlled experiments and interventions to test explanations.

Why do models make mistakes?

I explore interpretable failure detection: identifying model errors through evidence that can be inspected and checked.

What makes an explanation useful?

I am interested in explanations that are faithful to model behavior and help people assess it. Formal methods offer ways to make claims precise and checkable.
